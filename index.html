P(Disease | Evidence) = P(Evidence | Disease) × P(Disease) / P(Evidence)

Where Evidence = (Fever, Cough, Headache, Fatigue) and conditionally independent symptom likelihoods are multiplied under Naive Bayes conditional independence.
